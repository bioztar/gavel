Your part

Cloture — the motion that cuts off debate. Everything an agent cannot do for the Google Meet demo. Four lanes are running without you — listed at the bottom.

1. Pick the Google account that becomes Karen

You have several. Pick one you don't mind being seen in a meeting: the display name on that account is the name every participant reads in the Meet roster. MEET_BOT_NAME only applies to anonymous joins, and we join signed in.

  • Rename that account's profile to Karen (Google account → Personal info → Name) before the first login. Cosmetic — and it is the whole joke.
  • A plain @gmail.com works. A Workspace account is better if the meeting is hosted on the same Workspace: same-domain participants are usually admitted without anyone clicking "Admit".
  • Not your main account.

2. Make the signed-in profile (on your Mac)

The container has no screen, and Google's login form fights automation. So this happens once, by hand, on a machine with a display:

git clone https://github.com/bioztar/gavel.git    # if you don't have it
cd gavel/packages/ears-meet
just setup                                        # uv sync + Playwright Chromium
MEET_PROFILE_DIR=~/gavel-meet-profile just login

A visible Chromium opens at the Google sign-in page. Sign in as Karen, finish any 2-step prompt, press Enter in the terminal. The script confirms meet.google.com really shows the account menu, then closes.

That directory is the credential. It is a live Google session for the account. It is gitignored on both ends and must never enter an image, a commit, or a screen share.

3. Copy the profile to the VPS

scp -r ~/gavel-meet-profile coder@uk-lon-1:/home/coder/DEV/gavel/secrets/meet-profile
ssh coder@uk-lon-1 'chmod -R go-rwx /home/coder/DEV/gavel/secrets/meet-profile'

secrets/ is already in .gitignore — committed today in 19fde09. Profiles go stale after a long gap; if Meet asks to sign in again, redo step 2 and re-copy.

4. Four lines in .env on the VPS

MEET_URL=https://meet.google.com/xxx-yyyy-zzz
MEET_PROFILE_HOST_DIR=./secrets/meet-profile
EARS_WIRE_URL=ws://ears-meet:8787
EARS_HTTP_URL=http://ears-meet:8787

The last two are what swap the brain off Discord and onto Meet. Without them the stack boots fine and chairs a Discord call. Everything else has a default — see the MEET block in .env.example.

docker compose --profile meet up -d

5. The rehearsal needs a second human

  • Karen needs someone to interrupt and someone to cut off. One tab of yourself is not a rehearsal — turn-taking keys off other people actually speaking.
  • Book 30 minutes with one other person.
  • Record it. The pitch lives or dies on her timing, and the only way to tune timing is to watch it back.
  • Do it at least a day before the real pitch. A first live call with a browser-driving bot always surfaces something.
